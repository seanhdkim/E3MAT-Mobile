Calculation and Plot Notes
  • Equation A-1: ΔF0 = asnsRs + ainiRi.
  • The governing separation is the larger of the two desired/interferer calculations.
  • Pass/fail is evaluated against the unrounded governing result. The parenthetical result is the whole-MHz ceiling for reference.
  • The numerical plot is a planning visualization. It is not a measured transmitter spectrum, spectral-mask compliance determination, received power prediction, or independent interference analysis.
  • PCM/FM plotting defaults: randomized NRZ data, peak deviation of 0.35R, and a four-pole Bessel-magnitude approximation with an overall premodulation-filter -3 dB point of 0.7R.
  • Coded SOQPSK variants retain a SOQPSK-family shape and are widened using the applicable Table A-3 over-the-air bit-rate multiplier.
Coefficients and rate multipliers are implemented from RCC Telemetry Standards, Appendix A, Equation A-1, and Tables A-1 through A-3.
