Calculation Notes
  • Equation A-1: ΔF₀ = asnsRs + ainiRi.
  • The governing required separation is the larger result from the two desired/interferer directions.
  • Pass/fail is evaluated using the unrounded governing calculation. The parenthetical value is the whole-MHz ceiling for reference when frequency assignments are made in 1 MHz increments.
  • Results are planning guidance only. Validate final frequency assignments, receiver assumptions, operational constraints, and spectrum coordination requirements through the appropriate frequency-management process.
Coefficients and multipliers are implemented from RCC Telemetry Standards, Appendix A, Equation A-1, Tables A-1 through A-3.
